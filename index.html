07 / YOUR ASSETS. YOUR WALLET.SELF-CUSTODY BY DESIGN

Control begins with your wallet

Your assets
do not become
our balance.

Supported assets remain in a user's own wallet. Approved smart-contract operations can exchange eligible tokens, and resulting tokens return to that wallet. Permissions still carry risk.

An approval is authority, not a deposit.

A token allowance may let an authorized contract move approved assets without signing each trade. Review the spender, limit, and ability to revoke permissions before participating.

12 / RESPONSIBLE BY DESIGNUNDERSTAND BEFORE PARTICIPATING

A necessary part of the story

Intelligence is
not certainty.

There is no guaranteed profit, principal protection or elimination of investment loss. Automated systems can trade under unfavorable conditions. Explore each risk.

01Market & relative-price risk+

GLD and SLV-linked products can decline in value. Their relative relationship can change unexpectedly and strategy decisions can be unfavorable.

02Execution, liquidity & fees+

Slippage, price impact, pool liquidity, failed transactions, gas and exchange costs may reduce the value of a portfolio.

03Smart-contract & wallet risk+

Software bugs, vulnerabilities, network failures, wallet compromise and excessive allowances can lead to loss.

04Issuer, product & eligibility risk+

Stock Tokens are issuer-linked financial instruments, not physical metals or ETF shares. Issuer, legal, trading and regional restrictions apply.
